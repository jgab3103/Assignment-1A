Supporting Students Who Are Deaf or Hard of Hearing

Welcome

Figure 1. Strategies for setting up an inclusive classroom.

Welcome to our learning package on supporting students who are deaf or hard of hearing.

This resource has been developed for teachers and school staff who work with students who are deaf or hard of hearing in mainstream and inclusive classrooms. Its purpose is to build understanding of how hearing loss can influence learning, communication, participation and wellbeing, while providing practical strategies that support equitable access to education.

Students who are deaf or hard of hearing are a diverse group with different communication preferences, levels of hearing, and educational needs. Rather than focusing on limitations, this learning package explores how thoughtful classroom design and inclusive teaching practices can reduce barriers and enable students to thrive.

Throughout this package you will explore the challenges to engagement that students may experience, recognise the strengths they bring to the classroom, discover available supports, and learn practical teaching actions that promote inclusion and meaningful participation.

We encourage you to engage with the interactive resources, reflect on your own teaching context, and consider how small adjustments to classroom practice can make a significant difference for students who are deaf or hard of hearing.
