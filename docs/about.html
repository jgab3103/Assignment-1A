Supporting Students Who Are Deaf or Hard of Hearing

Welcome

Welcome to our learning package on supporting students who are deaf or hard of hearing.

This resource has been designed for teaching staff who may have a student with hearing loss or a student who is deaf in their classroom or school. It aims to build understanding of the experiences and challenges these students may encounter, while providing practical strategies that teachers can use to support participation, engagement and learning.

Across this learning package, you will explore common challenges faced by students who are deaf or hard of hearing, student strengths and strategies, available supports, and practical actions teachers can take to create a more inclusive classroom environment.

Our package incorporates a range of interactive digital learning objects to support different ways of engaging with the content. We encourage you to explore the resources, consider how the information relates to your own teaching context, and reflect on how small changes in classroom practice can make a meaningful difference.
